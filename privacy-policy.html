SIA privacy policy & data deletion

Pilot policy · Effective 2 October 2026

SIA Automobiles Pvt Ltd · SIA Relay Test WhatsApp service

Pilot service noticeThis policy covers a limited pilot. Deletion of pilot records follows the request process below. A processed relay event is not automatically deleted, and no automatic purge has been verified.

Who operates this service

SIA Automobiles Pvt Ltd operates SIA OS and its WhatsApp relay for a limited pilot to receive and review business enquiries and operational information. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact sales@siamotor.com.

Information we receive and use

When you contact the connected WhatsApp number, the service may receive your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, message text, message identifiers and timestamps. Meta webhook payloads can also contain delivery/status information, account events and metadata about media you send. Server logs may record technical request details.

We use this information to receive, route and review messages, keep pilot records, detect duplicates and check message authenticity. Local records may include a lead or conversation, held reply drafts and follow-up tasks. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information through this pilot service.

Storage and access

WhatsApp/Meta delivers webhook data to a Hostinger-hosted relay, which stores the raw payload and signature. SIA OS can import accepted information into its local database on the business PC. Marking a relay event as processed does not delete its stored payload.

Business access to pilot records is limited to Sagar Rana. Anyone else needs his approval. Meta and Hostinger also handle information as part of providing their services. This access rule does not mean provider access is eliminated. No promise of end-to-end encryption or encryption at rest is made for business-side copies.

For the current pilot, automatic customer sending and external AI drafting are disabled. This policy must be reviewed before those uses or the pilot's audience are expanded.

Retention

For this pilot, message records are to be kept for no more than 30 days and deleted earlier following a verified deletion request, except where retention is legally required. This rule is for pilot message records, not unrelated accounting or business records.

SIA has not yet verified how this rule applies to raw relay payloads, imported records, technical logs and backup copies. No automatic purge or backup-expiry process has been verified. Switching the service off does not itself remove stored records.

Request deletion of your data

Email sales@siamotor.com with the subject "SIA data deletion" and the WhatsApp number used to contact the service. Identify the conversation or approximate dates. Do not send passwords or identity documents with the initial request.

Sagar Rana reviews requests and may ask for limited information to verify that they relate to your data. Relevant records are to be removed within 7 working days, unless a record must be retained for a legal reason. Any necessary retention should be explained to you. Deleting WhatsApp from your phone does not delete these business-side copies.

Effective 2 October 2026. This policy covers the current pilot only and will be reviewed before its audience or uses are expanded.
